Privacy Policy for 3D Ludo Arena

Effective Date: November 2026

Shadiva Studio built the 3D Ludo Arena mobile game as an ad-supported 3D board game application offering Offline (Pass & Play, VS Computer) and Online Multiplayer game modes.

1. Information Collection and Use

3D Ludo Arena does not require mandatory user registration or collect sensitive personal documents. To provide core gameplay features, the app processes:

  • Local Game Data: Player display name, avatar selection, virtual in-game coins, level progress, and active match state are stored locally on your device using local storage.
  • Online Multiplayer Data: Temporary match room codes and gameplay state are synced in real time to connect players in online matches.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The app integrates trusted third-party services that may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage analytics to serve advertisements and maintain online multiplayer stability:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements.
  • Google Firebase: Used for real-time online multiplayer room matchmaking and game state synchronization.

3. Data Security & Children's Privacy

We do not sell user personal data to third parties. The game is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
